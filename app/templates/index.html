Train the models

Trains on your own data on this laptop. No internet and no API.

  1. Eight placement models and five marks models are trained and compared.
  2. Each score comes from repeated 5-fold cross-validation, so it is not luck from one split.
  3. Random Forest, Gradient Boosting and XGBoost are tuned inside each fold (nested validation).
  4. The best models are saved and the app switches to them automatically.

Ready. Training takes about one to three minutes.
